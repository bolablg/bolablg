### Profile

I spent nearly seven years at [Gozem](https://gozem.co), the African mobility, commerce, and fintech super app, progressing from the group's first data professional to Data Director:

- **Global Data Analyst · Nov 2019–Sep 2022:** established the data function and grew the initial team from 0 to 4
- **Head of Data · Oct 2022–Mar 2025:** scaled the organization from 4 to 14+ across analytics, engineering, and domain-aligned squads
- **Data Director · Apr 2025–Jul 2026:** owned group data and AI strategy, platform, products, governance, FinOps, people operations, budgets, contractors, and vendors

I am also open to consulting engagements. As a **Data Management Consultant** with the [UN International Trade Centre](https://www.intracen.org/) in 2023, I advised 15 early-stage ECOWAS startups on data strategy and coached five founders one-on-one.

### Track record

**Data & decision systems**

- Defined and governed cross-market KPIs for country managers, Finance, Operations, Product, and executive reporting
- Expanded self-service analytics to 12+ active managers and cut routine query demand on the central data team by about half
- Applied RFM, cohort, CAC/LTV, attribution, funnel, fraud, and marketplace analysis to operating and growth decisions

**Analytics engineering**

- Migrated 10+ TB and 50+ ELT/ETL pipelines to BigQuery in one quarter without interrupting operations
- Built a daily upsert system processing approximately 5 TB across Airflow and Dataform workloads
- Operated nearly 300 Dataform models and Airflow DAGs combined, with data-quality monitoring across hundreds of source and analytical tables
- Built reusable Dataform patterns for partition-aware MERGE, schema evolution, assertions, and business-rule tests
- Reduced Google Cloud cost by about 43% in one quarter while maintaining data freshness and integrity expectations

**Platform & applied AI engineering**

- Architected the Gozem Data Hub, a modular React and FastAPI platform serving 650+ employees and partners across five African operating countries and Singapore HQ
- Personally coded 17 of its 19 microservices, including a dynamic API gateway, centralized identity and RBAC, shared Google integrations, audit and notification capabilities, and production job orchestration
- Established reusable service standards for authentication, permissions, migrations, health checks, audit events, observability, retries, recovery, and deployment
- Built gated GitLab delivery pipelines with linting, automated tests, container builds, Trivy vulnerability scanning, staged promotion, releases, and failure notifications
- Shipped 30+ production AI and automation tools, including a Gemini document-extraction workflow that reduced a monthly five-country finance process from 1–1.5 weeks to under one day
- Built G-Force, a mobile-first operational application that onboarded roughly 700 field agents and reached about 200 daily users

**People & portfolio leadership**

- Built the data organization from 0 to 14+, managed eight domain leads, evaluated 50+ candidates, and onboarded 12 hires with structured 30/60/90-day plans
- Cataloged 100+ data and AI use cases, selected the top 15 for the roadmap, and aligned delivery with business leaders across the group
- Owned hiring, performance reviews, promotions, compensation recommendations, budgets, contractors, and vendor negotiations

**Research & community**

- Founding Vice-Chair, [iSheero](https://www.isheero.com/): developing AI and data talent in Benin and Francophone Africa
- Co-founded and chaired [Takwimu LAB](https://github.com/Takwimu-Lab), co-creating a 70,000+ sentence French–Fongbe/French–Ewe dataset: [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/4266935) · [AfricaNLP at EACL 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02516)
- Won the 2020 Zindi AI4D African Language Dataset Challenge with Takwimu LAB
