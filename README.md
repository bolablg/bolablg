## About Me

Statistician by training, data engineer and builder by practice. I lead Data & Analytics at [GOZEM](https://gozem.com), where I’ve scaled a 14+ person distributed team and built the "Data Hub" platform that supports business intelligence across 10+ countries in Sub-Saharan Africa.

I also consult — most recently with the **UN International Trade Centre** on data strategy for West African startups. Outside of work, I co-founded [Takwimu Lab](https://github.com/Takwimu-Lab) to advance NLP research for African languages, and currently serve as Vice-Chair of [iSheero](https://isheero.com), supporting AI talent in Francophone Africa.
