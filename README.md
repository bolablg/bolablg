### What I do

I design, build, and lead end-to-end data and AI systems. From raw infrastructure to production analytics, from hiring the first analyst to managing a distributed team of 14+ across multiple countries.

At [GOZEM](https://gozem.com) (the super app powering mobility, commerce, and fintech in Africa), I built the central Data Hub that serves business intelligence across the group. I cut google cloud costs by 43% while scaling the platform. Before that, I architected the data strategy, fraud detection systems, and cloud migration that became the foundation for everything the team runs today.

I also take on consulting work. Most recently, I partnered with the **UN International Trade Centre** to shape data strategy for several West African startups.
